A clear access process

Four steps to employer access

We want employer access to be straightforward while protecting organizations, candidates, and recruitment information.

01

Submit a request

Share your business details and the contact information of your authorized representative.

02

Business verification

TBBD reviews the organization and verifies the representative's relationship to the business.

03

Await approval

Your request remains pending while it is reviewed. Submitting a request does not grant employer permissions.

04

Access activation

After approval and role assignment, an authorized representative can access the permitted employer features.

Important: Employer registration and approval are not activated by this information page. Contact TBBD to discuss access while the protected request and approval workflow is being implemented.
