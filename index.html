WHO WE ARE

People. Technology. Performance.

Loyal Trade Management Ltd. is a Bangladesh-based Workforce and Technology Solutions company established in 2019.

We help organizations strengthen their workforce capabilities through Recruitment and Talent Acquisition, Workforce Solutions, Employee Onboarding, Employer Branding, and Human Resources (HR) and Human Capital Management (HCM) solutions.

As workforce management continues to evolve, we have expanded our capabilities by embracing cloud technologies, digital platforms, and modern workplace solutions, including Microsoft Azure-based technologies.

By combining workforce expertise with modern technology, we help organizations attract and manage talent, improve employee experiences, optimize workforce processes, and achieve better business outcomes.
